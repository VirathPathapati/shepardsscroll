ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

MORE THAN A SCHOOL PAPER.

Shepherd's Scroll is a student-led digital publication created to give the GSIS community a place to tell stories, explore ideas and start conversations.

WHY WE EXIST

A school is full of stories that rarely make it onto a timetable.

Shepherd's Scroll exists to give those stories a place. From student perspectives and personal experiences to science, culture, school life and the world beyond campus, the publication is a space for students to write about what interests them and what matters to them.

STUDENT-LED

The publication is created and developed by students, giving students an opportunity to write, edit, contribute and take part in the editorial process.

OPEN TO IDEAS

Shepherd's Scroll welcomes different subjects, perspectives and styles. A good story can come from anywhere.

EDITORIALLY REVIEWED

Every submission is reviewed before publication. Editing focuses on clarity, accuracy, grammar and presentation while respecting the writer's voice.
