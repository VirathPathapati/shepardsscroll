FROM THE EDITOR

There is always
another story.

Every school has its official story. It lives in assemblies, announcements, photographs, achievements and the events that make it onto the calendar.

But that is never the whole story.

There are conversations that happen after class, ideas that begin in a corridor, questions that never get asked out loud, people whose work quietly keeps everything moving and experiences that can change the way we see our own school.

Shepherd's Scroll exists for those stories.

It is a place for students to write about what they notice, what they believe, what they create and what they want others to think about. It can be serious, curious, unexpected or simply worth sharing.

Because student life is not one story. It is hundreds of them, unfolding at the same time.
